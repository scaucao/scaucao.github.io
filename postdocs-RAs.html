Postdocs and Research Assistants

I have been fortunate to supervise and collaborate with postdoctoral researchers, research assistants, and visiting interns on projects in numerical analysis, finite element methods, and stationary and nonstationary partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics and related areas.

I would be happy to discuss potential research opportunities with prospective postdoctoral researchers, as well as possible applications to national or international postdoctoral fellowships.
