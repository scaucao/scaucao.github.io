Thesis Students

I have had the privilege of supervising several outstanding young mathematicians during their PhD and Master's studies, as well as undergraduate thesis projects.

Prospective students are warmly encouraged to contact me to discuss potential research opportunities in numerical analysis, finite element methods, and the study of stationary and time-dependent partial differential equations arising in fluid mechanics and related areas.
